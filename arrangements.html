Pokhara flights: the buffer is deliberate

Domestic flying saves a long road journey, but weather can cancel flights. The FCDO also flags aviation-safety concerns for Nepal’s airlines. Read that advice when choosing transport; a road journey has its own risks. A road fallback can take a full day or longer and depends on landslides and traffic. Check conditions before committing to it. FCDO · transport & trekking ↗

Ask the trekking agency for a written package

Route, people & accommodation

  • Two travellers; seven walking days, 7–13 October. Confirm the actual sleeping elevations, especially Low Camp, Badal Danda and High Camp.
  • Named licensed guide, language, experience on this ridge and first-aid training; optional porter, fair agreed load and staff insurance/equipment.
  • Reserve six teahouse nights: 7 Deurali, 8 Forest, 9 Low, 10 Badal, 11 High, 12 Low. Agree private/twin rooms where available and shared-bathroom expectations.
  • Agree the upper viewpoint as the default objective and the price of an extra day or early descent.

Permits, money & contingency

  • Ask the agency to issue an agency TIMS card and ACAP permits before 7 October. NTB explicitly lists Mardi Himal under its guide/TIMS rule; do not rely on blog claims that no guide is needed.
  • Current published foreigner fees: ACAP NPR 3,000 + TIMS NPR 2,000 per person; NPR 10,000 total for two before any agency service charge.
  • Specify three meals/day, accommodation, guide/porter costs, transfers, taxes, permits, emergency coordination and cancellation policy. Check tea, boiled water, charging, Wi-Fi and showers separately.
  • Record rescue contacts and the insurer’s authorisation procedure; establish check-in messages and what happens if mobile reception fails.

Entry, mobile data & luggage

Nepal visa: cover both visits

A 30-day multiple-entry tourist visa is the sensible validity to discuss for 26 September–17 October; the official fee is US$50 per person. The elapsed trip is longer than 15 days even though you leave for Bhutan.

Use Nepal Immigration’s official process, carry your first hotel address and onward ticket, and check the entry stamp’s validity and re-entry entitlement. An online application alone is not proof a visa has been granted.

Nepal Immigration · visa options ↗

Connectivity without surprises

Ncell sells tourist SIMs at KTM airport with the original passport; tourist eSIMs are also offered online. Choose validity through 17 October, check phone compatibility/unlocking, and test data before leaving the counter. Avoid a plan that expires while you are in Bhutan.

For Bhutan, ask Breathe Bhutan to help obtain a visitor SIM at Paro airport or a Bhutan Telecom/TashiCell outlet. Confirm whether the device supports the supplied format. Keep your Nepal SIM for the return.

Download maps and documents first. Neither mobile reception nor lodge Wi-Fi should be relied on for rescue.

Three airlines, three baggage checks

Your THAI tickets show 1 checked piece; their attached economy note states 23 kg/piece. Drukair tickets show 30 kg checked, while its published economy cabin limit is 5 kg. Follow ticket-specific allowances and reconfirm discrepancies with the carrier.

Pokhara fares may have a smaller checked allowance: verify the actual fare before buying. Keep power banks and spare lithium batteries in cabin baggage and check airline restrictions.

Keep the trek bag light

Arrange one secure storage point at the post-trek Lakeside hotel, or have the agency transfer sealed main bags there from Sarangkot. Obtain a receipt and keep passports, cash and valuables with you.

Ask Breathe Bhutan how Bumdra overnight bags are carried, which sleeping gear is supplied and where larger bags stay in Paro. Your Nepal trekking porter is a separate arrangement.

Final confirmations with Breathe Bhutan

The package lists hotels, three meals, private transport/guide, SDF, Bhutan visa processing, entrance fees and return Bhutan flights. Alcohol, shopping, guide/driver tips and horse riding are excluded; luxury dining outside the stated arrangement may also cost extra. Avoid paying SDF or Bhutan flights a second time.
